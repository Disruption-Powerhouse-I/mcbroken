👋 Hello, builder! This book explains how the McBroken app works using simple words and pictures. Every page has a real grown-up secret hidden inside it. If you read the whole book, you will know how to build a real phone app — and how to keep it happy for two whole years!

1 The One Big Question

Imagine you really, really want a McFlurry. 🍦 You drive all the way to McDonald's… and the machine is broken! Oh no! What a sad day.

McBroken is an app that answers one question, super fast:

"Can I get my frozen treat at THIS McDonald's right now?"

That's it. No long stories. No pictures of food. No chatting. You open the app, you look, you decide. Open, look, decide! 🚀

💡 Did you know? There are more than 13,000 McDonald's restaurants in the United States! That's a lot of ice-cream machines to keep an eye on. We can't watch them all ourselves — so kind people called Flurry Scouts help us. (More about them soon!)

2 The Four Frozen Friends

Every McDonald's has four cold treats that we watch. We call them the Four Frozen Friends:

🍦 McFlurry
🥤 Milkshake
☕ Frappe
❄️ Frozen Coke

We always show these four friends in the same order, like four good buddies standing in a row. We call it the Sacred Row. No matter which store you look at, the row looks the same — so your eyes always know where to look. 👀

📚 Big Word Alert: "Component" Grown-up engineers call each frozen friend a component. It just means "one of the four things we keep track of." When you hear "component," picture a frozen friend! 🥤

How does a friend change its mood? With a simple counting game! 🔢

How many people said "it's broken!"Mood
Nobody (0)🟢 Chillin' — everything is fine
One person (1)🟠 Sus — one person had trouble
Two or more people (2+)🔴 Cooked — uh oh, it's really down
💡 The clever secret We only count different people. If you tap "broken" two times, that still counts as just one person. So nobody can trick the app by tapping a bunch of times! This is how we keep the app honest. 🤝
📚 Big Word Alert: "We don't store the mood" Here's a grown-up trick: the app does not save the mood anywhere. Instead, it counts the reports fresh every single time you look. This means the mood is always correct and never gets stuck on the wrong color. Engineers call this "computing status on read." Fancy! ✨

4 The Magic 24-Minute Timer

Ice-cream machines get fixed all the time! So an old report shouldn't make a store look broken forever.

That's why every report has a magic timer. After 24 minutes, the report goes poof 💨 and disappears. If all the "broken" reports disappear, the frozen friend goes back to happy Chillin' green.

🎯 Try this thought: Think of each report like an ice cube. 🧊 As soon as someone drops it, it starts melting. After 24 minutes, it has melted away completely — gone! Only the fresh, un-melted ice cubes count toward the mood.

We also show how fresh the news is, like "7m ago" (which means "7 minutes ago"). We never show long, boring history lists. Just the fresh scoop! 🍨

5 Your Secret Scout Name

When you open McBroken for the first time, something magical happens. You don't have to type your name. You don't need an email. No password. Nothing!

Instead, the app gives you a secret field ID — a cool code name like:

🥄 Flurry Scout ID: K7QX-91RM

This is your spy name as a Flurry Scout! It stays with you, but it never tells anyone who you really are. We don't know your name, and we don't want to. Your secret is safe. 🤫

📚 Big Word Alert: "Anonymous" Anonymous means "nobody knows who you are." McBroken is anonymous on purpose. We collect no names, no phone numbers, no nothing. You're just a brave, mysterious scout. 🕵️
⚠️ A tiny secret for grown-ups Behind the curtain, the app quietly makes sure one person can't make a hundred fake scout names to cheat. It uses a special phone "fingerprint" (called App Attest and Play Integrity) that's totally free. But shhh — kids and users never see this. It just works. 🪄

6 The "Are You REALLY There?" Rule

This is the most important rule in the whole book. Are you ready? 🥁

You can only report about a store if you are actually AT that store.

Why? Because if someone far away could say "the McFlurry is broken," they might just be being silly or mean. But a scout who is really standing there knows the truth! That's what makes McBroken trustworthy. 💪

So the app checks your location. If you're close enough (about as far as a football field, around 150 meters), it says "In range ✅". If you're too far, it says "Not in range ⚠️" and politely won't let you report.

📚 Big Word Alert: "Geofence" A geofence is an invisible bubble drawn around the store. 🫧 If you're inside the bubble, you can report. If you're outside, you can't. The server (the big brain in the cloud) draws the bubble and decides — not your phone. That way nobody can fib about where they are.
⚠️ No location = no McBroken If someone says "no, you can't know where I am," then sadly they can't use the app. That's okay! The whole app only works because scouts are really there. Our polite message says: "No location, no McBroken. We only accept intel from people actually at the store."

8 The Cloud Brain Does the Thinking

Here's a golden rule of McBroken: your phone is not allowed to make important decisions. Your phone is just a pretty window. 🪟 The cloud brain (the server) makes all the big choices:

  • "Is this scout really at the store?" → the brain decides. 🧠
  • "What mood is the McFlurry in?" → the brain counts and decides.
  • "Is this person allowed to report?" → the brain decides.

Why? Because a sneaky person could try to trick a phone. But the cloud brain has its own copy of where every store is, so it can't be fooled. The phone just shows what the brain says. 😇

📚 Big Word Alert: "RLS" RLS stands for Row Level Security. It's a set of strong locks 🔒 on our data. The locks let scouts read the store moods, but they do not let scouts sneak in and change things directly. All changes must go through the brain's front door, where the guard checks everything.

9 The Three Kinds of Intel

"Intel" is a spy word for "useful news." Scouts can send three kinds:

TypeWhat it meansTaps
🚨 Crowd Intel"I couldn't get my treat!" (Something's broken.)1 tap
🧾 Crowd Clear"Actually, it's working fine!" (Cancels a broken report.)1 tap
🍟 Crew WordA real McDonald's worker tells the truth. The most trusted news of all!2 taps

No typing! No long messages! No photos! Just a tap or two. Easy-peasy. 🫰

🎯 The counting magic We take the "broken" reports and subtract the "it's fine!" reports. If more people say fine, the friend goes back to green. The crowd fixes its own mistakes! 🧮

10 Crew Word: The Super-Trusted Helpers

The people who work at McDonald's know the real truth about the machines. So we let them become special Verified Crew for their own store. 🍟

How does a worker become crew?

  • They tap "I work here" on their store's page.
  • They type a secret store code (the manager gives it to them).
  • They must be at the store (geofence again!).
  • Ta-da! They get a 🍟 Verified Crew badge — but only for that one store.

When crew speak, it looks like an official stamp:
"🍟 Crew Word: Frappe is Chillin' (6m ago)."

Crew Word is the boss! It beats all the crowd reports for 24 minutes. Because who knows better than the person standing at the machine? 🧑‍🍳

12 Taking Care of the App for 2 Years 🌱

Building an app is like planting a little tree. You can't just plant it and walk away — you have to water it and care for it so it grows strong. Here's how 3 to 5 friends keep McBroken healthy without getting tired.

🗓️ The Caring Calendar

How oftenWhat we do
Every day (robots do it!)Robots test new code and sweep away ice cubes older than 25 minutes. We don't lift a finger. 🤖
Every week (~1 hour)One friend (we take turns!) checks for broken things and makes sure we're still inside the free zone.
Every month (~2 hours)The whole team looks at how the app is doing and fixes any sneaky bugs.
Every 3 monthsWe refresh the big list of McDonald's stores and practice rescuing our saved data.
Twice a yearWe update our building blocks (Expo) so nothing gets old and creaky.
Once a yearWe pay Apple again, do a big safety check, and make sure we follow all the app-store rules.
💡 The "Take Turns" rule With only 3–5 friends, we take turns being the "helper of the week." That one person watches for problems, and everybody else gets to keep building fun things. Next week, someone else takes a turn. Nobody gets too tired, and everybody learns how everything works! 🔄

🧹 Keep the Toys Fresh

Phone apps get old fast! Apple and Google change their rules every year. So we use a helpful robot called Renovate that raises its hand 🙋 whenever a building block has a newer version. We never fall too far behind — that's the #1 way little apps stay alive.

🛟 Always Have a Safety Net

Every day, a robot makes a copy of our important lists (the stores and the scouts) and hides it somewhere safe and free. And every 3 months, we practice bringing it back — because a safety net you never test is just a rumor! 🪂

🐷 The Piggy Bank Plan

As long as fewer than ~50,000 people use the app each month, we stay free! When more people come (yay!), we'll happily pay about $25 a month for a bigger cloud brain. We even made a list of "warning lights" that tell us exactly when it's time to upgrade. Growing up costs a little — and that means McBroken is winning! 🏆

🎯 The 1-in-5 promise Out of every 5 days of work, we spend 1 day cleaning up and making things better — not just adding new stuff. It's like tidying your room a little every week so it never becomes a giant mess. 🧼
